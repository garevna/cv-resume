- **Real-time sync:** WebSocket connection with automatic reconnection (exponential backoff) for live seat availability and booking status updates
- **Localization:** uk/en/de via vue-i18n with date, currency, and phone number formatting per locale
- **Authentication:** Firebase Auth (email/password, SMS+reCAPTCHA, Google OAuth)
